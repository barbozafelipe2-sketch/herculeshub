# Branch policy

**Canonical branch: `main` only.**

- Do not create, use, or maintain additional branches for Hercules Hub unless Felipe explicitly authorizes an exception.
- Make approved work directly on `main` after reviewing existing state and validating the change.
- Before removing any existing branch, verify that its unique work is preserved on `main` and keep the branch commit SHA in the audit record.
- Keep experimental Decision Trace / PR-13 work clearly labeled as QA-only and separate from the commercial production app.

This repository-level policy records Felipe's operating instruction. It does not automatically change branch settings or other repositories.
